At Saysh, we believe women deserve better —in footwear, in representation, and in opportunity. That’s why we’re celebrating the groundbreaking partnership between Always Alpha , founded by Allyson Felix, and Deep Blue Sports and Entertainment . This joint venture will elevate female athletes by securing commercial opportunities and reshaping the women’s sports marketing landscape.
