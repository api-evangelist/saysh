Dear Women, As I write this letter, I stand on the shoulders of my mother, grandmothers, aunties, and countless women who have gone before me. Women who have blazed trails of inequality so that I can run the path before me with strength and grace. Because of these women, I hold hope and an unshakeable belief that Women Deserve Better.
