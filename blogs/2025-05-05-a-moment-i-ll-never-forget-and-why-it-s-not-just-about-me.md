Today, I received an honor I never imagined when I first laced up a pair of spikes as a shy teenager in Los Angeles: I’m being inducted into the United States Olympic & Paralympic Hall of Fame. Even writing that feels surreal. I think back to the beginning—the early mornings, the heartbreaks, the tiny moments that didn’t feel important until they shaped who I became.
