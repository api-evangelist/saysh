“This is the story of the birth of Spike One.” Saysh’s story begins with Allyson leaving her then-sponsor Nike in 2018. Allyson wasn’t simply standing up for herself but for her fellow teammates and athletes who too may have aspired to have a family and not have it held against them. She was standing up for the next generation of athletes who deserve to have it all: a family and a career and not feel like they have to choose between one or the other in order to support themselves and make a living.
